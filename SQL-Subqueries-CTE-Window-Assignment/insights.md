# Brief Insights

## 1. High-value sales orders

The query for above-average sales helps identify orders that performed better than the overall average order value. These orders are useful for understanding which products or customers contribute more revenue.

## 2. Customer-level sales performance

Using CTEs, total sales were calculated for each customer. This helps identify the customers who generate the most revenue for the business.

## 3. Customer ranking

The RANK window function was used to rank customers based on their total sales. This makes it easier to find top-performing customers.

## 4. Order-level customer behavior

The ROW_NUMBER window function was used to assign row numbers to each customer’s orders. This helps analyze the order sequence and highest-value orders within each customer.

## 5. Top and bottom customers

Top customers can be targeted for loyalty programs, special offers and retention strategies. Bottom customers can be studied to improve engagement or understand why they purchase less.

## 6. Single-order customers

Customers with only one order may need follow-up campaigns, discounts or better engagement to encourage repeat purchases.

## 7. Above-average customers

Customers whose total sales are above the average customer sales are valuable for business growth and should be prioritized for retention.
