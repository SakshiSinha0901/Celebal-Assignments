# Customer Sales Insights

This file contains the key insights generated from the SQL analysis of the Superstore sales dataset.  

The analysis is based on Subqueries, CTEs, Window Functions, JOINs, and aggregate functions.

---

## 1. `01_above_average_sales.csv`

This file contains all orders where the sales value is greater than the overall average sales value.

### Insight

These orders are performing better than the normal order value in the dataset.  
They help identify high-value transactions and can be useful for understanding which products, customers, or regions are contributing more revenue.

This type of result is useful when the business wants to focus on stronger sales records instead of looking at every small transaction.

---

## 2. `02_highest_order_per_customer.csv`

This file shows the highest sales order for each customer.

### Insight

Every customer may have placed multiple orders, but this result highlights the best order made by each customer.

This helps understand the maximum value each customer has contributed in a single purchase.  
Some customers may not order frequently, but if their highest order value is high, they can still be important for the business.

---

## 3. `03_total_sales_per_customer.csv`

This file contains the total sales generated by each customer.

### Insight

This gives a customer-level summary of sales performance.  
Instead of checking individual orders, we can clearly see how much total revenue each customer has contributed.

Customers with higher total sales are valuable for the business and can be considered for loyalty programs, special offers, or retention campaigns.

---

## 4. `04_above_average_customer_sales.csv`

This file shows customers whose total sales are greater than the average customer sales.

### Insight

These customers are performing better than the average customer in terms of total sales.

This is useful because it separates stronger customers from the overall customer base.  
The business can focus more on these customers because they already show good buying behavior and higher revenue contribution.

---

## 5. `05_customer_sales_rank.csv`

This file ranks all customers based on their total sales.

### Insight

The ranking makes it easy to compare customers by sales performance.

Customers with lower rank numbers are the highest-performing customers.  
This helps the business quickly identify top customers without manually comparing total sales values.

Ranking is useful when the business wants to prioritize its most valuable customers.

---

## 6. `06_order_row_number_per_customer.csv`

This file assigns row numbers to orders within each customer using a window function.

### Insight

The row number helps organize each customer’s orders in a proper sequence based on sales value.

For every customer, the order with row number 1 is the highest sales order for that customer.  
This makes it easier to study customer order behavior and identify the most important order for each customer.

---

## 7. `07_top_3_customers.csv`

This file shows the top 3 customers based on total sales.

### Insight

These are the three highest revenue-generating customers in the dataset.

They are very important from a business point of view because they contribute more sales compared to other customers.  
The business can focus on retaining these customers through better service, personalized offers, or priority support.

---

## 8. `08_final_combined_query.csv`

This file contains the final combined result using JOIN, CTE, and Window Function together.

### Insight

This result shows customer name, total sales, and sales rank in one final view.

It combines customer details with calculated sales performance.  
This is useful because it gives a clean and complete customer sales summary that can be directly used for business analysis.

This query also shows how multiple SQL concepts can be used together to solve a practical business problem.

---

## 9. `09_top_5_customers.csv`

This file shows the top 5 customers based on total sales.

### Insight

These customers have generated the highest total sales in the dataset.

They are high-value customers and should be treated as important business assets.  
The company can use this information to create customer retention plans, loyalty benefits, or exclusive offers for these customers.

---

## 10. `10_bottom_5_customers.csv`

This file shows the bottom 5 customers based on total sales.

### Insight

These customers have generated the lowest total sales in the dataset.

This does not always mean they are unimportant.  
They may be new customers, one-time buyers, or customers who have not been engaged properly.

The business can study these customers to understand why their sales contribution is low and whether there is a chance to improve engagement.

---

## 11. `11_single_order_customers.csv`

This file contains customers who placed only one unique order.

### Insight

These customers have interacted with the business only once.

This is useful because one-time customers can be targeted for repeat purchase campaigns.  
The business can send offers, reminders, or better product recommendations to encourage them to place more orders.

Converting single-order customers into repeat customers can help improve customer retention.

---

## 12. `12_highest_order_value_per_customer.csv`

This file shows the highest order value for each customer.

### Insight

This result helps identify the maximum single-order purchase made by every customer.

It is useful for understanding each customer’s highest spending capacity.  
A customer with a high maximum order value may be a good target for premium products or special sales campaigns.

This insight is different from total sales because it focuses only on the highest individual order, not the customer’s complete purchase history.

---

# Overall Conclusion

This assignment helped analyze customer sales performance using SQL.

The main business insights from the analysis are:

- High-value orders can be identified using above-average sales.
- Total sales per customer helps find the most valuable customers.
- Customer ranking makes comparison easier.
- Top customers can be targeted for retention and loyalty programs.
- Bottom customers and single-order customers can be studied for better engagement.
- Highest order value helps understand each customer’s maximum purchase capacity.
- The final combined query gives a clean customer sales summary with total sales and rank.

Overall, Subqueries, CTEs, Window Functions, and JOINs helped convert raw Superstore data into useful business insights.
